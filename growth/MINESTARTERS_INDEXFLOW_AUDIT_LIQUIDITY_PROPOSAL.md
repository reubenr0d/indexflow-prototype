# Minestarters Strategic Proposal: IndexFlow Audit and Liquidity Seed

Status: draft board proposal  
Date: 2026-05-26

---

## Executive summary

Minestarters should fund a staged audit and liquidity seed for IndexFlow as strategic infrastructure for the licensed stock-vault business Minestarters intends to launch after receiving its Daba license.

This is not a proposal for Minestarters to publicly launch unlicensed stock vaults today. It is a proposal for Minestarters to secure priority access, strategic economics, operational readiness, and audit-backed infrastructure before the license arrives.

IndexFlow can launch as independent, permissionless financial infrastructure. Minestarters can fund the work through a private strategic agreement that gives Minestarters future benefits without presenting the first public launch as a Minestarters investment product.

Recommended approval: **up to $1.25m**, released in milestones.

---

## Strategic rationale

Minestarters' future licensed stock-vault business will need more than a trading thesis. It will need audited vault infrastructure, reliable valuation, redemption controls, market data integrations, custody-compatible workflows, and operational history.

IndexFlow gives Minestarters a path to build that foundation now:

- **Audit readiness before licensing:** When the Daba license is granted, Minestarters can launch on audited infrastructure rather than starting a 6-12 month security and deployment process.
- **Atlas execution layer:** IndexFlow becomes the onchain execution and accounting layer for future Atlas-driven mining, stock, and thematic strategies.
- **Proof for regulators and partners:** Live infrastructure, documented controls, NAV history, oracle policy, and redemption operations strengthen future licensing, custody, and LP conversations.
- **Strategic rights:** Minestarters can receive priority launch rights, fee economics, token/equity exposure, or service credits in exchange for funding the audit and initial liquidity.
- **Market option value:** If IndexFlow succeeds as independent infrastructure, Minestarters benefits commercially even before launching its own regulated vault products.

The core argument: **Minestarters is not buying a marketing campaign. It is buying down future execution, audit, compliance, and time-to-market risk.**

---

## What IndexFlow is

IndexFlow is infrastructure for structured exposure products:

- users deposit USDC into basket vaults
- vaults issue transferable basket shares
- vault managers can allocate capital into a shared perpetual liquidity layer
- NAV is calculated onchain from idle reserves, allocated capital, and position PnL
- redemption quality is explicitly separated from portfolio value

This matters because Minestarters' future products will likely be judged on both investment performance and operational integrity. IndexFlow gives the product line an auditable accounting and settlement layer instead of a black-box offchain workflow.

---

## Regulatory posture

The first IndexFlow launch should be positioned as independent protocol infrastructure, not as a Minestarters-branded public stock-vault product.

Recommended posture:

- **IndexFlow:** independent, permissionless protocol infrastructure.
- **Minestarters:** strategic funder and future preferred operator.
- **Public stock vaults:** deferred until Minestarters has the required license or operates through a properly licensed partner.
- **Near-term Minestarters usage:** internal testing, technical validation, closed pilot capital, and future launch preparation.

This avoids the weak framing that Minestarters is launching public stock vaults under a "trial" label. The substance should be: Minestarters is preparing infrastructure for a regulated product line.

## Funding request

Approve up to **$1.25m** in staged funding for audit, launch readiness, and controlled liquidity seeding.

### Stage 1: Audit readiness and external audit

Recommended budget: **$200k-$350k**

Scope:

- smart contract audit covering basket vaults, share accounting, vault accounting, oracle path, GMX fork integration, funding rates, pricing, and cross-chain redemption flow
- pre-audit cleanup and internal test hardening
- audit-fix verification
- production deployment controls: multisig, timelock, monitoring, alerting, incident response
- initial legal/product memo for the IndexFlow-only launch posture
- initial bug bounty reserve

Why this matters: the system handles user deposits, vault shares, oracle pricing, leveraged synthetic exposure, PnL attribution, and redemption liquidity. A lightweight audit is not enough for board-grade infrastructure.

### Stage 2: Controlled liquidity seed

Recommended budget: **$500k-$900k**

Scope:

- seed USDC for the shared perp liquidity layer
- small basket-vault operating capital
- redemption reserve buffer
- LP or market-maker incentive reserve
- keeper, monitoring, and operations runway

Purpose: prove the system with real capital while keeping risk contained. The goal is not vanity TVL. The goal is to validate deposits, NAV calculation, redemptions, keeper updates, liquidity behavior, and operational controls.

## What Minestarters gets

Minestarters should not fund this as a donation. The funding should be tied to a strategic agreement with clear upside.

Recommended benefits:

- **Priority launch rights:** first right to launch Minestarters-branded licensed stock, mining, and thematic vaults once legally cleared.
- **Economic participation:** protocol fee share, token warrant, equity warrant, or service-fee credit.
- **Atlas integration rights:** preferred integration path for Atlas signals, research, and agent-managed strategy workflows.
- **Data rights:** access to operational data, NAV history, liquidity behavior, and risk reports for future licensing and investor materials.
- **Governance seat or observer rights:** visibility into protocol roadmap, audit remediation, asset admission, and launch sequencing.
- **Brand option:** ability to publicly associate with IndexFlow after counsel confirms the right timing and wording.

Suggested commercial structure:

> Minestarters funds the audit and seed liquidity under a strategic infrastructure agreement. In return, Minestarters receives priority operator rights, future economic participation, and preferred Atlas integration rights. Public Minestarters-branded vault launches remain deferred until license approval or a licensed-wrapper arrangement is in place.

---

## Why now

Waiting until the Daba license arrives creates three risks:

1. **Time-to-market risk:** Minestarters receives the license but still needs months of audit, remediation, liquidity setup, and operational proving.
2. **Partner risk:** custodians, LPs, market makers, and institutional allocators will ask for audited infrastructure and operational history before committing.
3. **Category risk:** another protocol or manager can occupy the onchain structured-stock-vault narrative first.

Funding IndexFlow now gives Minestarters a ready lane when the license lands.

---

## Risk controls

Recommended controls before any liquidity release:

- audit report delivered and reviewed
- no unresolved critical findings
- multisig ownership and timelock deployed
- production oracle plan approved
- clear risk limits for initial liquidity
- no public Minestarters-branded stock-vault deposits before license clearance
- public messaging reviewed by counsel
- weekly board reporting during the first 90 days after launch

---

## Board decision requested

Approve a staged strategic funding commitment of up to **$1.25m** for IndexFlow audit, launch readiness, and controlled liquidity seeding.

The first release should be capped at **$250k** for audit kickoff and readiness work. Further releases should depend on audit progress, remediation status, launch controls, and board-approved liquidity risk limits.
