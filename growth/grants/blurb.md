# IndexFlow -- Grant Application Blurb

---

IndexFlow is a structured exposure protocol that lets asset managers, RWA issuers, and chain ecosystem teams launch basket vaults backed by shared perpetual liquidity.

Deposits are routed toward chain deployments with deeper reserves and stronger redemption capacity, making reserve depth the protocol’s core growth lever.

This creates a clear chain flywheel: ecosystem support increases reserves, deeper reserves attract more vault deposits, higher deposits drive TVL, volume, and fees, and those metrics justify further reserve expansion.

Each deployment is ring-fenced with its own TVL, volume, fee, and reserve metrics, so grants and incentives can be tied to attributable chain growth.

IndexFlow is not a generic perp DEX or passive wrapper. It is infrastructure for custom structured products with configurable baskets, custom oracles, manager-defined logic, and chain-specific liquidity expansion.
